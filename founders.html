Our story

How we got here

A short history of a soap works that refused to cut corners.

The beginning

A natural-oil soap works

While much of the industry built its bars on animal-fat oils, RAJU ENTERPRISES committed to plant-based oils from the start — the hallmark that set our detergent soaps apart.

Growth

Raju Gota goes global

Our flagship bar found shelves well beyond India, reaching South Africa, Nepal and other export markets, sold through everything from small retail stores to large supermarkets.

Today

Raju Washing Powder

As households moved from bars to detergents, we followed. Our newest product carries the same formidable capability and quality standard as Raju Gota.

Inside the unit

Precision at production scale

Our facility runs at a 5 tonne daily capacity and produces 3 tonnes of soap a day — with accuracy and quality checks built into every batch.

Quality first

Rigorous testing on every batch, so performance never drifts between orders.

Plant-based only

Not a trace of animal-derived oil enters any product we manufacture.

Made to order
