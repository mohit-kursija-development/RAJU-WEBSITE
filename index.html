FAQ

Washing soap questions, answered

What buyers most often ask about our soap, bulk supply and export.

What is Raju Gota washing soap made of?
Raju Gota is made exclusively from plant-based oils. Unlike much of the laundry soap industry, RAJU ENTERPRISES has never used animal-fat oils in any of its detergent soaps or washing powder.
Do you supply washing soap in bulk or wholesale?
Yes. Raju Gota and Royal Gota soaps ship 10 packs per box (850 g packs) or 50 pieces per box for single 120 g bars, and Raju Washing Powder ships 10 packs per box. Contact us with your volume for pricing.
Can I order custom soap sizes and packaging?
Yes. For large-scale orders we customise bar weight, unit count per pack, wrapper artwork and carton configuration to suit the price point and brand you sell under.
Which countries does RAJU ENTERPRISES export to?
Our flagship Raju Gota is exported to several markets including South Africa and Nepal, alongside distribution across India through both small retail stores and large supermarkets.
How much soap can you manufacture per day?
Our Ulhasnagar facility has a daily manufacturing capacity of 5 tonnes and consistently produces 3 tonnes of soap per day.
Where is RAJU ENTERPRISES located?
