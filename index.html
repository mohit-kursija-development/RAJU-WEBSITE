Our story

Four decades of natural-oil soap

RAJU ENTERPRISES has manufactured laundry soap for over forty years, and never once with animal-fat oils. As households moved from bars to powder, we followed — Raju Washing Powder carries the same formula strength as Raju Gota.

  • Exclusively plant-based oils in every formulation
